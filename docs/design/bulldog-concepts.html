Meet the candidates 🐾

Three bulldog directions, drawn in the exact medium he'll be built in (layered SVG). Each shown on light + dark backgrounds. The bandana/collar always takes your partner colors.

A · Mochi the Marshmallow

Recommended

light  ·  dark

Cream chibi blob with huge glossy eyes, squishy jowls, forehead wrinkles, blush, tongue-peek, and the partner-color bandana. Maximum baby-schema cuteness — the face is 70% of him, built to emote.

  • happy: jowls jiggle + ears flap on the hop
  • boop: whole-face squish, eyes squeeze to arcs
  • sleepy: eyes → sleepy lids, body slow-breathes
  • eyes track your finger (pupil offset)

B · Sir Wrinkles

Most "bulldog"

light  ·  dark

Fawn coat, eye patch, heavy brow, droopy eyes, wide stance and the signature underbite with two little teeth. Grumpy-cute: he looks unimpressed until you check in — then the teeth grin gets you.

  • happy: stoic → sudden full-body wiggle (contrast = comedy)
  • pout: the champion pouter — brow drops, jowls sag
  • proud: chest out, tag sparkles
  • collar tag doubles as milestone medal

C · Bean

Minimal kawaii

light  ·  dark

The whole dog is one soft bean — dot eyes, w-mouth, nub ears, a heart on his hip. San-X/Molang energy. Least effort to animate beautifully; cuteness comes from timing, not detail.

  • happy: boings like jelly (squash & stretch)
  • boop: ripples like poked pudding
  • love: hip-heart floats up and pops
  • rolls instead of walks

All three use the same layered rig (ears / eyes / brows / jowls / mouth / body / tail as separate groups), the same mood store already in the app, Reduce-Motion falls back to static poses, and the bandana/collar/neckerchief is drawn from both partner colors. Mixing is allowed — e.g. Mochi's body with Sir Wrinkles' underbite teeth.
